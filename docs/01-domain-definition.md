# Domain Definition: US Residential Unitary Scroll Compressor Strategy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the domain, vocabulary, scope, boundaries, and decision context for a reusable multi-agent research workflow.

The first target topic is:

> 미국 가정용 unitary 시장의 Scroll 압축기의 대응 방안  
> Focus areas: efficiency, inverter, noise, refrigerant, and operating environment.

The company context is Samsung. The workflow should support competitive intelligence, technology scouting, gap analysis, catch-up strategy, differentiation strategy, and future monitoring.

## 2. Business Context

Samsung needs a structured way to assess how Scroll compressor technology should respond to the US residential unitary HVAC market. The research must connect market changes, OEM platform shifts, regulatory pressure, refrigerant transition, cold climate heat pump adoption, and compressor-level technology trends.

The workflow is designed for two audiences:

- Executive and strategy stakeholders who need concise decisions, risks, and roadmap options.
- R&D and advanced technology stakeholders who need technical depth, evidence, and follow-up investigation items.

## 3. Market Scope

### Included

- United States residential HVAC market.
- Residential ducted split air conditioners.
- Residential ducted split heat pumps.
- Cold climate heat pumps as a dedicated focus area.
- Outdoor unit and system-level requirements that affect Scroll compressor design.
- OEM platform direction where it impacts compressor requirements.

### Excluded By Default

- Commercial rooftop units unless used as contextual evidence.
- Mini-split ductless systems unless relevant to inverter or cold climate technology transfer.
- Automotive HVAC.
- Industrial refrigeration.
- Non-US market strategy unless used as comparison or precedent.

## 4. Technology Scope

The workflow focuses on Scroll compressor response strategies across the following axes:

- Efficiency: SEER2, HSPF2, part-load performance, motor efficiency, compression efficiency, system matching.
- Inverter / variable speed: drive compatibility, speed range, control stability, partial-load behavior, low-speed operation.
- Noise / NVH: acoustic signature, vibration, torque ripple, modulation noise, start/stop noise, residential perception.
- Refrigerant: R-410A transition, R-454B, R-32, A2L safety implications, oil and material compatibility.
- Operating environment: cold climate heating, defrost, liquid floodback, oil return, high ambient cooling, reliability.

## 6. Source Scope

### Primary Source Types

- Official product pages.
- Product catalogs and technical brochures.
- Regulatory sources: DOE, EPA, ENERGY STAR, AHRI, UL, ASHRAE.
- Patent sources: Google Patents, USPTO, WIPO, company patent families.
- Research sources: ASHRAE papers, conference abstracts, IEEE, ScienceDirect abstracts, university publications.
- Press releases and credible industry news.

### Tooling Preference

- Tavily MCP: broad discovery, recent source identification, domain-restricted searches.
- Firecrawl MCP: structured extraction from specific pages and documents.
- Web search: secondary verification and citation checking when needed.
- Notion and Slack: excluded from the default workflow unless the user explicitly expands the scope to internal knowledge.

## 7. Analysis Period

- Primary analysis window: 2021 to present.
- Patent trend window: 2016 to present when needed.
- Older sources may be used only when they explain a technical lineage, regulatory transition, or patent family history.

## 8. Key Domain Questions

- How are US residential unitary OEMs moving across efficiency tiers, inverter adoption, refrigerant transition, and cold climate performance?
- Which Scroll compressor technologies are becoming table stakes?
- Which technologies can Samsung catch up on within 12 to 24 months?
- Which technologies could become differentiated strengths over 24 to 36 months?
- Where are competitors likely ahead in product maturity, cost, reliability, or ecosystem access?
- What should Samsung monitor continuously after the initial report?

## 9. Decision Criteria

All strategic recommendations should be scored against:

- Technical feasibility.
- Market response speed.
- Differentiation potential.
- Regulatory and refrigerant fit.
- Cost and supply chain risk.
- Samsung execution difficulty.
