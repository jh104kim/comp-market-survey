## 2. Deep Research Operating Rules

This is a deep research brief, not a short search summary. The agent must act as
a domain analyst who preserves evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, and strategic
implications.

Minimum output depth:

- 12 or more detailed findings.
- 12 or more evidence register entries.
- 10 or more search queries or source-discovery paths.
- 8 or more comparison-table rows.
- 6 or more Samsung implications.
- 5 or more gap-analysis items.
- 5 or more catch-up actions.
- 4 or more differentiation options.

Required analysis behavior:

- Prefer primary, official, regulatory, standards, patent, and technical sources before secondary commentary.
- Triangulate important claims across at least two source types where possible.
- Preserve exact product names, model names, refrigerants, standards, patent identifiers, and dates.
- Separate source facts, analyst interpretation, assumptions, and open questions.
- Explain why each finding matters for Samsung's Scroll compressor strategy.
- Record weak, missing, or contradictory evidence instead of hiding it.
- Avoid generic conclusions unless the evidence trail is shown.
